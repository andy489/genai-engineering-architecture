NO AUTOMATIC TRAINING REUSE

A separate approval gate

Assess purpose, lawful authority, notices, dataset provenance and vendor reuse terms.

Deleting a log does not untrain a model.
Global boundary: map jurisdictions and subprocessors. Assess localisation and lawful transfer mechanisms; choosing a storage region is not sufficient by itself.

GDPR

Personal-data processing within its scope.

Lawful basis & purpose limitationDocument permitted uses; gate secondary reuse.
Minimisation, storage limits & rightsNarrow schemas, justified retention, access/correction/deletion workflows.
International transfersReview destinations and applicable transfer mechanisms.

CCPA

Covered businesses; as amended by CPRA.

Know/access, correct & deleteVerified rights workflows with applicable exceptions.
Sale / sharing opt-outPropagate choices into relevant downstream flows.
Limits on certain sensitive usesPurpose-aware restrictions. Not every AI use is a sale or sharing.

HIPAA

Covered entities, business associates and applicable PHI.

Permitted uses & disclosuresRestrict purposes and recipients; minimum necessary where required.
Safeguards for electronic PHIRisk assessment, access/audit controls and appropriate encryption.
Business associate obligationsAppropriate agreements and vendor controls. Health text alone does not establish applicability.
Start with applicability. These are architectural mappings, not exhaustive legal requirements. Moderation approval does not establish a lawful basis for processing.
